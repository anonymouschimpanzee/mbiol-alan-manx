**Summary**
This repository contains code used to process, analyse and plot data used in the project. The files are split up by Objectives 1-4. Within each is the step-by-step code detailed in the Methods section of the write-up. 

**Objective 1: To evaluate the impact of ships on sky brightness** 
	⁃	Hypothesis 1: Sky brightness increases with the number of ships present in the area
	⁃	Hypothesis 2: Sky brightness is inversely related to the distance between ships and the colony, with closer ships increasing sky brightness more (Distance)
	⁃	Hypothesis 3: Sky brightness increases when ship lighting is oriented toward the colony compared to when it is oriented away (Heading)

**Objective 2: To determine whether light and ships impact activity levels at the breeding colony**
	⁃	Hypothesis 4: The number of shearwaters on the surface of the colony is significantly lower when sky brightness is higher 
	⁃	Hypothesis 5: Vocal activity is significantly lower when sky brightness is higher (AudioMoth processing and evaluation)

**Objective 3: To determine whether light levels and ships impact breeding birds and subsequently, chick provisioning behaviour**
	⁃	Hypothesis 6: Colony attendance of breeding shearwaters is significantly lower on nights with brighter skies (Geolocator read-in and mixed effects model)
	⁃	Hypothesis 7: Chicks are less likely to be fed by adults on nights with brighter skies (Meal size calculation)
	⁃	Hypothesis 8: Chicks are fed smaller meals on nights with brighter skies 

**Objective 4: To evaluate whether shearwater behaviour varies within a night in response to short-term fluctuations in light levels**
	⁃	Hypothesis 9: Shearwaters wait for the moon to set before arriving on the colony
